## Get up and running in three steps

Whether you're onboarding a small NGO team or deploying across a large field programme, Terriqon is designed to be operational quickly. Here's the essential flow from sign-up to your first approved report.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start your free trial">
    Go to [terriqon.com](https://terriqon.com) and click **Start Free Trial**. No credit card required — your 14-day trial begins immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build your form and deploy to your team">
    Use the drag-and-drop form builder to create your data collection form, then invite your Field Officers and assign them to a project.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Collect, sync, and approve">
    Your team submits data from the field — online or offline. Submissions auto-sync when connectivity returns, the AI drafts your report, and you review and approve it before it's downloadable.
  </Step>
</Steps>
