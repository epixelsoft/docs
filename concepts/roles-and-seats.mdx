Terriqon uses three roles to match access to responsibility. Your **Organization Admin** owns the account and billing. **Managers** run projects — building and assigning forms, reviewing submissions, and approving reports. **Field Officers** are the people on the ground collecting data. Your plan's seat limit is shared by your active Managers and Field Officers, while the Organization Admin never uses a seat. Understanding how roles and seats interact keeps your team organized and your costs predictable.

## The Three Roles

| Role | What they can do | Uses a seat? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Organization Admin** | Full account control: billing and subscription, user management, drag-and-drop form builder, project creation, report approval, data export. One per account. | No |
| **Manager** | Build and assign forms, manage projects, review submissions, review and approve reports, monitor the team | **Yes**, when active |
| **Field Officer** | Submit data via the PWA app, online or offline | **Yes**, when active |

<Info>
  There is one Organization Admin per account, and they never use a seat. Every active Manager and active Field Officer occupies one seat — so plan for both when you size your team.
</Info>

## How Seats Are Counted

Your plan's seat limit is the number of **active** members you can have at once — Managers and Field Officers combined, in any mix. For example, the **Field** plan includes 15 seats: you could run 3 Managers and 12 Field Officers, 15 Field Officers and no extra Managers, or any other combination up to 15 active members. The Organization Admin sits outside this count.

Every Manager and Field Officer is in one of three states:

- **Active** — can sign in and use Terriqon, and occupies one seat.
- **Archived** — kept on your account with their full profile and history, but **cannot access the platform or the mobile app** and does **not** use a seat.
- **Pending** — has been invited but hasn't accepted yet. Pending invites do **not** use a seat, so you can queue people in advance without eating into your cap; the seat is only consumed once they accept and become Active.

Archiving is how you free a seat without losing anyone's data. Alongside your active seats, each plan lets you keep an archived roster of the same size — so on the Field plan you can have up to 15 active members and up to 15 archived members. Reactivate an archived member at any time, as long as you have a free active seat.

You can review your current seat usage at any time from **Settings → Billing**.

## Managing Seats

**To free up a seat**, archive a Manager or Field Officer who is no longer active:

1. Go to **Team** and select the member.
2. Choose **Archive**. They keep their profile and history but lose access to Terriqon, and their seat is released for someone else.

Reactivate them later from the same screen whenever you have an open seat.

**To add more seats**, upgrade your plan from **Settings → Billing → Change Plan**:

| Plan | Active seats (Managers \+ Field Officers) | Archived roster |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Field | 15 | Up to 15 |
| Ops | 50 | Up to 50 |
| Command | 150 | Up to 150 |

If you are on the Command plan and need capacity beyond 150 seats, contact your account manager to discuss a custom arrangement.

The Organization Admin never counts toward your seat total. Only **active** Managers and Field Officers use seats — archived members of either role do not.
