Terriqon offers three plans designed to scale with your organization — from small field teams to enterprise operations. Every plan gives your team access to the full Terriqon platform, including offline data collection, real-time KPI dashboards, and AI-generated reports. The Field and Ops plans both include a 14-day free trial with no credit card required, so you can validate the platform before committing.

## Plan Comparison

<Note>
  Your **Organization Admin** owns billing and doesn't use a seat. **Managers and Field Officers share your plan's seat cap** in any mix — on the Field plan, for example, you could run 3 Managers and 12 Field Officers, or 15 Field Officers and no extra Managers. Each plan also allows an equal-sized archived roster, so you can keep former team members on record without losing seat capacity. See [Roles & Seats](/concepts/roles-and-seats) for the full model.
</Note>

## What's Included in Every Plan

Every Terriqon plan — regardless of tier — includes the full core platform:

- **Drag-and-drop form builder** — design and deploy data collection forms with no coding required
- **Offline PWA** — field officers collect data without an internet connection; submissions sync automatically when connectivity returns
- **Real-time KPI dashboards** — monitor field activity, KPI progress, and submission volume as data comes in
- **GPS activity map** — visualize where your field officers are working and track submission locations
- **Data export** — download your submission data for external analysis or record-keeping
- **Standard email support** — reach the Terriqon support team at [support@terriqon.com](mailto:support@terriqon.com)

## Add-Ons: Scale Without Changing Plans

If you outgrow your plan mid-cycle, you don't have to jump to the next tier. Terriqon offers two add-ons you can buy on top of your existing plan:

- **Additional seats** — extra active seat capacity beyond your plan's cap, for organizations that briefly need more team members than their tier includes.
- **AI report packs** — extra AI reports beyond your monthly allowance, useful during heavy reporting cycles (quarter-end, grant deadlines).

Purchase either from **Settings → Billing** in the Terriqon dashboard. Both add-ons appear on your next invoice alongside your regular plan charge, and you can adjust or cancel them at any time.

## NGO Discount

Terriqon offers a verified discount for registered non-governmental organizations on annual billing plans.

To apply for the NGO Verified Discount:

1. Choose an annual billing plan (Field Annual or Ops Annual).
2. Email [support@terriqon.com](mailto:support@terriqon.com) with the subject line **"NGO Discount Request"** and attach proof of your organization's NGO status (such as a registration certificate or tax-exemption letter).
3. The Terriqon team verifies your status and applies the discount to your account, typically within 2 business days.

<Info>
  The NGO Verified Discount is available on annual billing only and cannot be combined with other promotional offers. Contact [support@terriqon.com](mailto:support@terriqon.com) if you have questions about eligibility.
</Info>

## Free Trial

The Field and Ops plans include a 14-day free trial — no credit card required. You get access to the full platform for your selected plan tier from day one.

- **No auto-charge at trial end.** When your trial expires, your account is paused rather than automatically billed. You must affirmatively choose a paid plan to continue.
- **Full platform access.** Your trial includes every feature of your chosen plan — offline PWA, AI reports, KPI dashboards, everything.
- **Command trials** are available on request. Contact [support@terriqon.com](mailto:support@terriqon.com) to arrange a guided Command trial with your dedicated Customer Success Manager.

<Tip>
  Switch to annual billing at any time from the billing portal to immediately apply the 20% discount to your next payment.
</Tip>
