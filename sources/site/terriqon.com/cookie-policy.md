This Cookies Policy explains how Epixel Software Private Limited ("EPixelSoft," "Terriqon," "we," "us," or "our") uses cookies and similar technologies on terriqon.com and within the Terriqon platform. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy, which explains more broadly how we handle personal data.

## 1\. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website or web application stores on your device when you visit. They let the site remember things about your visit — like whether you're logged in, or how you got to a page — so it doesn't have to ask you again on your next click. We also use similar technologies, like local storage, for some of the same purposes; for simplicity, we refer to all of these collectively as "cookies" in this Policy.

## 2\. The Cookies We Use, By Category

We keep the number of cookies we use to what's actually needed to run and improve Terriqon. Here's the category-level picture:

| Category | What It's For | Set By | Optional? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Strictly Necessary | Keep you logged in, remember your session, protect against fraud and abuse. | Terriqon (first-party), Cloudflare, Stripe (checkout only) | No |
| Analytics / Performance | Understand how the Platform and website are used. | PostHog | Yes |
| Functional / Support | Power the live chat widget. | Crisp | Yes |
| Payment | Enable secure checkout, prevent payment fraud. | Stripe | No, while completing a purchase |

Stripe's cookies are only activated when you access billing or checkout functionality, not site-wide. Cloudflare cookies depend on which specific Cloudflare security products are enabled for our site; some are strictly necessary only in that context, not universally.

## 4\. Your Consent

Where applicable law — including the GDPR for visitors in the EU/EEA — requires consent before we activate non-essential cookies, we are implementing a consent mechanism designed to ensure that analytics (PostHog) and functional (Crisp) technologies are not activated until you have made a choice through that mechanism, with separate controls for each category, no pre-ticked boxes, and a persistent "Cookie Settings" link to change your mind at any time. Until that mechanism is fully deployed on our site, please use the browser and provider-level controls described in Section 5 to manage non-essential cookies — we do not currently treat continued browsing alone as consent, but we also do not yet have an on-site preference center actively blocking these cookies pending your choice. Strictly necessary cookies do not require consent, as they are essential to providing the service you've requested by visiting or using the Platform.

We do not currently respond to browser "Do Not Track" signals or Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals. If we implement support for either in the future, we will update this section to reflect that.

## 5\. How to Manage or Disable Cookies

You have a few ways to control cookies:

Browser settings: Most browsers let you block or delete cookies, or ask for your permission before a site sets one. Since browser menus vary, check your browser's help documentation for exact steps (usually under "Privacy" or "Site settings").

Provider-level opt-outs and policies: You can review or opt out directly through each provider's own tools and policy: Stripe, Cloudflare, PostHog, Crisp.

What happens if you block cookies: Strictly necessary cookies can't be turned off without breaking core functionality, like staying logged in. Blocking analytics or functional cookies won't stop you from using the Platform, but may disable the live chat widget or reduce the quality of our usage insights.
