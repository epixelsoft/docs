# Cancellation & Refund Policy

A product of Epixel Software Private Limited

Effective Date: July 1, 2026  ·  Version 1.1

This Cancellation & Refund Policy ("Policy") explains how Epixel Software Private Limited ("EPixelSoft," "Terriqon," "we," "us," or "our") handles subscription cancellations, plan downgrades, refunds, and account credits for the Terriqon platform. This Policy is incorporated into, and forms part of, our Terms of Service. Capitalized terms not defined here have the meaning given in the Terms of Service.

## 1\. Our Approach, in Plain Terms

We designed this policy around five principles:

- Monthly changes take effect at the end of the billing month. If you downgrade a monthly plan, you keep your current plan and full access until your current month ends — you are never cut off mid-cycle.
- Annual changes take effect immediately. Holding an annual customer on a plan they've decided to leave for up to a year isn't something we think is fair, so annual downgrades apply right away, with the unused portion of what you paid returned to you as described below.
- Downgrades are settled with account credit, not cash. When you downgrade (as opposed to cancelling outright within a refund window), the value of what you've already paid but haven't used is returned to you as account credit rather than a bank refund. Credit never expires and is applied automatically to future invoices, though it is not redeemable for cash and is forfeited if your account is permanently closed. Cash refunds are reserved for early cancellations, within the windows described below, and for billing errors.
- We never delete your data merely because you downgraded. Your projects, reports, submissions, and field officer records are fully preserved on a downgrade. If your account is fully cancelled or terminated, Customer Data is retained for an export period and then deleted on the schedule described in our Privacy Policy — it is not kept indefinitely.
- We configure our billing systems to apply this Policy consistently. If you ever believe your billing history doesn't reflect this Policy, contact us and we will investigate.

## 3\. What Counts as "Purchase" or "Renewal"

Throughout this Policy, the refund and downgrade windows described below (3 days for monthly plans, 30 days for annual plans) are measured from whichever of the following most recently occurred: (a) your initial paid subscription purchase; or (b) your most recent annual renewal charge. Reactivating a previously cancelled subscription is treated as a new purchase for this purpose. Purchases made through a sales-assisted Order Form follow the same windows measured from the Order's effective start date, unless the Order states otherwise.

## 4\. Monthly Plans

### 4.1 Downgrading a Monthly Plan

You can request a downgrade at any time from the billing portal. The downgrade is scheduled for the end of your current billing month — you keep your current plan's features and limits until then, and no charge or credit is issued at the time you request it. On your next billing date, you'll be charged at the new, lower plan's rate.

### 4.2 Cancelling a Monthly Plan

If you cancel within 3 days (72 hours) of your original purchase timestamp, you'll receive a full cash refund of that charge and your access ends immediately. If you cancel after that window, no refund is issued, but you keep access for the rest of the billing month you've already paid for; your account is then cancelled and access ends.

## 5\. Annual Plans

### 5.1 Downgrading an Annual Plan

Annual downgrades take effect immediately — you move to the new plan the same day you confirm the change. What happens to the unused portion of your annual payment depends on timing:

- Within 30 days of your annual purchase or most recent renewal: you receive a prorated cash refund for the unused months, calculated on a daily basis, back to your original payment method.
- After 30 days: the unused prepaid value of your current term, calculated on a daily basis, is issued as non-expiring account credit and applied automatically to your invoices under the new plan until exhausted.

### 5.2 Cancelling an Annual Plan

The same 30-day distinction applies to cancellations. Within 30 days of purchase or renewal, you receive a prorated cash refund and your account closes immediately. After 30 days, no refund or credit is issued, but you retain access for the remainder of your paid annual term before your account closes.

### 5.3 Upgrades

Upgrading to a higher plan takes effect immediately and is not subject to the cancellation windows in this Section 5, which apply to downgrades and cancellations only. On upgrade, we charge the prorated difference for the remainder of your current billing period; any existing account credit is applied automatically to that charge before your payment method is charged.

## 6\. How Account Credit Works

Account credit is issued as a balance on your Terriqon billing account (technically, a Stripe customer balance). When your next invoice is generated, any available credit is automatically deducted from the amount due before your payment method is charged — if the credit fully covers the invoice, you won't be charged at all; if it only partially covers it, we charge the remaining balance. Credit is non-transferable to another organization, is not redeemable for cash, never expires while your account remains open, is shown in your billing portal, and requires no action from you to redeem. If your account is permanently closed, any remaining credit is forfeited, except where applicable law requires otherwise.

## 7\. Billing Errors and Duplicate Charges

If you're charged in error — for example, a duplicate charge for the same billing period — contact us at support@terriqon.com and we will investigate promptly. Once confirmed, we issue a full cash refund of the erroneous charge to your original payment method, generally reflected within Stripe's standard processing time of 5–10 business days.

## 8\. Service Level Credits (Command Plan Only)

The Command plan includes an uptime guarantee, with the measurement period, calculation method, exclusions (such as scheduled maintenance and force majeure events), claim process, and credit cap set out in the applicable Service Level Agreement for the Command plan. In the event of a confirmed outage that breaches the guarantee, we issue an account credit calculated proportionally to the length of the confirmed outage, applied to your next invoice. This is a service credit, not a cash refund, and applies only to the Command plan; other plans do not carry a formal uptime SLA.

## 9\. Your Data, Access, and Fees Not Covered by This Policy

We do not delete organization data as a result of a downgrade. If a downgrade puts you over a lower plan's limits — for example, more active projects than the new plan allows — your existing projects and data remain fully readable, but creation of new projects beyond the limit is paused until you're back within it. Similarly, if your active Field Officer seat count is above your new plan's included seats, we don't automatically suspend anyone; instead, the excess is billed at your plan's standard per-seat overage rate until you free up seats or upgrade. Full seat and role mechanics are described in our Terms of Service. Following full account cancellation or termination, Customer Data is retained for an export period and then deleted according to the schedule in our Privacy Policy — see Section 7 of that Policy for exact timelines.

Previously generated Excel and Chart JSON report exports remain downloadable for 30 days after a downgrade away from a plan that includes those formats, even if your new plan doesn't include them going forward. After 30 days, new download links for those specific formats are no longer issued for that plan, though the underlying report data itself is never deleted for that reason.

This Policy covers Subscription Plan fees. Unless otherwise stated in an Order, professional services, custom development, migration, training, and onboarding fees are non-refundable once the applicable work has commenced. Refunds and credits described in this Policy are calculated on subscription fees actually received by us and do not include reimbursement of taxes already remitted to a tax authority, currency conversion differences, or third-party payment processing fees, except where applicable law requires otherwise.

## 10\. Illustrative Example

The figures below are hypothetical and for illustration only — they do not reflect actual Terriqon pricing. Please refer to our pricing page for current plan rates.

Suppose a customer on an annual plan paid $2,400 upfront for a 12-month term, and requests a downgrade 100 days into that term (after the 30-day window):

- Days remaining in the term: 265 of 365.
- Credit issued = (265 ÷ 365) × $2,400 ≈ $1,742.
- This $1,742 is applied as non-expiring account credit, automatically offsetting the customer's invoices under the new, lower-priced annual plan (charged at its full price for a fresh 12-month term) until the credit is used up.

Had the same customer requested the downgrade on day 20 (within the 30-day window), they would instead receive approximately $2,268 (calculated on 345 days remaining) as a cash refund to their original payment method, rather than as account credit.
