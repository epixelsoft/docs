WHY TERRIQON

## Built for reports that actually go to real people.

![Your data never reaches AI unprotected. Icon](https://terriqon.com/images/icons/landing-page/why-terriqon-icons/LockIcon.svg)

### Your data never reaches AI unprotected.

Names, phone numbers, GPS coordinates, and IDs are stripped before any AI processing. A full log of what was removed is kept for your records. Always.

![If the data is not good enough, we say so. Icon](https://terriqon.com/images/icons/landing-page/why-terriqon-icons/CheckIcon.svg)

### If the data is not good enough, we say so.

Too few submissions. Too many blank fields. The system detects low-quality data and routes to human review instead of generating a report that could mislead a donor or a board.

![Every report needs a human to approve it. Icon](https://terriqon.com/images/icons/landing-page/why-terriqon-icons/ShieldIconn.svg)

### Every report needs a human to approve it.

No AI report can be downloaded or shared without a named manager approving it first. If it is rejected, a written reason is required. The full trail is permanent.
